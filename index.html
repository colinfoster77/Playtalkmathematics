Welcome to Play Talk Mathematics

Welcome to our website for early years practitioners!

Our aim is to provide a wide range of practical and mathematically rich activities, all grounded in research evidence.

In designing the activities, we have focused on four key principles of early mathematics development: play, talk, adapt and manipulate.

Play

Guided play can be a powerful support for children’s learning,1 offering meaningful opportunities to engage learners, while still allowing children agency in their mathematics learning.2 Our activities provide a variety of stimulating scenarios for playful mathematical exploration.

Talk

Classroom interaction enables children to share and develop their thinking, offering support for learning within mathematics.3,4 Our activities provide opportunities for rich and sustained discussions, supporting sense-making, reasoning, problem solving and metacognition.

Adapt

Adaptation and flexibility are important components of effective pedagogy, ensuring teaching is sensitive to children’s developmental needs.5,6 Our activities can be adapted to suit individual need, providing optimal challenge for any child whatever their prior mathematical experiences.

Manipulate

Using manipulatives is a prevalent approach to support active mathematics learning.7 The use of concrete objects during hands-on activities can support mathematics learning8 and facilitate play-based pedagogies.9 Our activities involve children physically doing things - moving themselves or other objects around.

These tasks aim to reveal age 3-6 children’s current mathematical understandings and support their ongoing development. To see how these principles work in practice, explore our activities.
